T Approved A SEPARATE rule set from the Guiding Principles workbook, run independently of the model. It never alters any model number — margin, probability, EV and unit sizing are unchanged whether or not a game is approved. A game is approved when it passes every automatable hard rule (no 5+ pt line move, no side flip, Hawaii only at home, returning QB and no HC change on the picked team, never against Army/Navy/Air Force, no Week 0, no Friday, no Baylor, no bowls) Favourable classifications (upswing, overlooked, juggernaut, sleeper, QB/run combo, elite defense) and affirmative endorsements (Hawaii at home) are shown as TAGS for context — they are not required for approval, since the workbook never states that they are. Three principles are not automatable and are excluded: no-chasing, the eye test, and the spontaneous-Saturday rule. The badge cites the strongest single ground for approval; expand any card to see the full list of rules cleared, or which rules blocked it.
Units Selective allocation, not per-game Kelly. Ranks the slate by model edge, takes at most 3 games, and requires a 4.0-point minimum edge (~90th percentile) so a weak slate produces few bets or none. Stakes scale proportionally to edge within the selected set, capped at 2u per bet and 5u per slate. Based on professional practice: handicappers describe releasing "one pick or three, and sometimes passing on the entire slate." Selectivity limits downside — it does not establish that the model is right.
Units Suggested stake, 1u = 1% of bankroll. Quarter-Kelly sized, capped at 2%. Sized on the SHRUNK edge, and the shrinkage is inherited from an earlier different model rather than measured on this one (see below) — so "NO BET" here means "this model is unvalidated and the inherited prior says don't size it," not "we measured this bet as bad."
Line movement Badge beside the pick shows how the line has moved since first recorded. GREEN (down arrow) = moved toward our side, meaning we would have gotten a worse number waiting. RED (up arrow) = moved against us. Expanded card shows opening line, current line, and total movement.

Pts edge is how far the model's projected margin differs from the market line. Cover and Win Prob / Model EV are the model's own raw outputs — no shrinkage applied. These are what the model believes, not a validated forecast. Why the numbers look confident: raw Kelly sizing on these figures would recommend 41 of 51 games and 63.8 units — roughly 64% of bankroll in one week. Historical simulation of that approach went bankrupt in 95%+ of runs. That is why selection and sizing are handled separately from the displayed probabilities (see Units).
This model has never been validated. No 2026 games have been played, and this exact formula has never been tested against real results. The figures shown are the model's own raw outputs. A different, earlier model (a two-stage SP+ regression) showed no edge across 6,324 historical games — that is reason for caution, but it is not a measurement of this model. Its true edge is unknown, not zero.
